WHO WE ARE

Built Around Practical Engineering Experience

ALPH TECH is an Abuja-based industrial machinery and technical solutions brand serving clients across Nigeria. We support businesses with equipment selection, procurement, installation, commissioning, repairs, maintenance, factory planning and operator training.

Our experience covers packaging machinery, water and beverage production equipment, industrial and domestic water treatment systems and related technical services.

ALPH TECH operates in association with ALPHSUNDOR INTEGRATED SERVICES NIG LTD, providing practical technical support for clients who need machinery and production solutions.
