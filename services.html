OUR SERVICES

Professional Packaging Machine & Industrial Technical Services

ALPH TECH provides practical engineering and technical support for packaging machinery, food and beverage production equipment, water production systems and factory operations. We support businesses from the initial planning and selection of machinery through procurement, installation, commissioning, operator training, troubleshooting, repairs and maintenance. Whether you are establishing a new factory, expanding an existing production line or experiencing problems with a machine already in operation, we help you identify suitable technical solutions for your production requirements. Based in Abuja, Nigeria, we support businesses seeking dependable machinery services and production equipment expertise.

01
CONSULTATION

Technical Consultation for Machinery & Production Projects

Starting a production business or investing in new machinery requires careful technical planning. Selecting the wrong equipment can result in unnecessary expenditure, production difficulties, unsuitable packaging, or machines that cannot deliver the output a business expects. ALPH TECH provides technical consultation to help entrepreneurs, investors, factory owners and existing manufacturers make informed decisions before committing funds to machinery and production projects.

We help clients assess their proposed products, production targets, packaging formats, available workspace, power supply, water requirements where applicable, and the equipment needed to achieve their objectives. We explain important machine specifications, production capacity considerations, automation options and the relationship between individual machines within a complete production line.

Our consultation covers a range of production applications, including sachet water, bottled water, yoghurt drinks, flavoured beverages, liquid filling, food packaging and other manufacturing activities. We also assist businesses that already operate production equipment but need technical guidance on recurring faults, equipment upgrades, machine replacement or improvements to their existing production arrangements.

Where equipment is being sourced internationally, we can help clients review technical requirements and prepare questions for suppliers before placing an order. This helps clarify important matters such as machine compatibility, electrical specifications, spare parts, installation requirements and after-sales technical support.

Our objective is to help you understand your technical requirements, evaluate available options and develop a practical direction for your project before making major machinery investment decisions.

02
PROCUREMENT

Packaging Machine Procurement & Equipment Sourcing

Finding the right machine is an important step towards establishing an efficient production operation. ALPH TECH assists businesses with identifying and sourcing packaging and production machinery based on their product type, required output, packaging format, operating environment and budget considerations. Our approach begins with understanding what you intend to produce and identifying equipment specifications that suit your requirements.

Our equipment sourcing support covers vertical form-fill-seal (VFFS) packaging machines, sachet water packaging machines, liquid filling machines, bottle-blowing machines, shrink-wrapping machines, coding and marking machines, sealing equipment, automatic weighing and filling machines, and related food and beverage production machinery.

We help clients understand important selection factors, including machine speed, filling accuracy, packaging material compatibility, product characteristics, level of automation, electrical requirements, available installation space, spare parts availability and the technical support required after delivery. These considerations are particularly important when purchasing machinery from overseas suppliers.

Where applicable, we can assist with supplier communication, technical specification reviews, equipment comparisons and coordination of procurement arrangements. We also consider how the selected equipment will fit into the client's planned production process and what may be required to install and operate it successfully.

Our aim is to help clients make better-informed equipment choices and prepare for the next stages of installation, commissioning and production. Whether you need one replacement machine or equipment for a new production facility, ALPH TECH can help you assess the appropriate options for your project.

03
FACTORY SETUP

Factory Layout, Production Planning & Production Line Setup

A properly planned production facility makes it easier to position machinery, move materials, organize work areas and maintain equipment. ALPH TECH provides technical planning support to businesses establishing new factories or reorganizing existing production spaces. We help clients consider how their equipment, utilities, personnel and production activities should fit together before installation begins.

Our factory layout and production planning support includes assessing available space, considering machine dimensions, identifying practical equipment positions and planning the movement of raw materials, packaging materials and finished products. We also consider access for operators, equipment servicing, cleaning activities and the safe movement of people around the production area.

We support projects involving sachet water and bottled water factories, yoghurt and flavoured drink production facilities, liquid filling operations, food packaging businesses and other manufacturing applications. Depending on the project, planning may involve production machines, filling and sealing equipment, conveyors, coding machines, water treatment systems and associated equipment.

We help clients identify the equipment required for their intended production process and consider how different machines will connect operationally. Important planning considerations include electrical supply, water supply where required, drainage, ventilation, equipment foundations or support requirements, maintenance access and the handling of packaging and finished products.

We also help clients identify technical issues that should be addressed before equipment arrives. Final layouts, building requirements and utility specifications depend on the actual machinery, the facility, the production process and applicable safety and regulatory requirements.

Whether you are setting up your first production facility or expanding an established business, ALPH TECH can help you plan the equipment arrangement and production workflow before moving into installation and commissioning.

04
INSTALLATION

Packaging Machine Installation & Production Equipment Setup

Correct machine installation is essential for reliable operation, consistent product packaging and safe production. ALPH TECH provides installation and setup services for packaging machinery and selected production equipment. We help businesses position, assemble, connect and prepare their machines according to the equipment design, manufacturer instructions and site requirements.

We place particular emphasis on vertical form-fill-seal (VFFS) packaging machines used to package suitable powders, granules and other products in bags or pouches. Depending on the machine model, installation may involve positioning and levelling the machine, checking assemblies, arranging the packaging film path, inspecting sealing components, checking sensors and confirming that relevant electrical and control connections are correctly configured.

Our installation support also covers sachet water packaging machines, bottle-blowing machines, liquid filling machines, shrink-wrapping machines, coding and marking equipment and related production machinery. The installation process varies according to the equipment type, manufacturer specifications and operating environment.

We assess relevant site conditions and utility requirements before proceeding with equipment setup. Depending on the project, this may include reviewing electrical supply, machine earthing, compressed air requirements, water connections and the space needed for normal operation and maintenance.

Proper installation also provides an opportunity to identify loose assemblies, unsuitable connections, alignment concerns and other issues that may affect subsequent commissioning. We address installation-related concerns within the agreed scope of work and identify issues requiring additional specialist attention where necessary.

If you have purchased a new machine or relocated an existing production unit and need technical assistance to install and prepare it for operation, ALPH TECH can support the installation process in Abuja and, subject to project arrangements, other locations across Nigeria.

05
COMMISSIONING

Machine Commissioning, Calibration & Test Running

After installation, a machine must be checked and tested to confirm that its components and operating functions work as intended. ALPH TECH provides commissioning and test-running support to help businesses prepare their packaging and production equipment for normal operation. This stage helps identify incorrect settings, operational inconsistencies and technical issues that need attention before production proceeds.

Depending on the machine, commissioning may involve checking operating sequences, configuring relevant parameters, verifying sensors and control functions, checking temperature settings, reviewing filling mechanisms and testing moving components. We also assess equipment operation against the applicable manufacturer specifications and the requirements of the intended product.

For VFFS packaging machines, attention may be given to packaging film movement, bag formation, filling operations, sealing temperature, cutting mechanisms and the coordination of different machine functions. These settings must be appropriate for the specific machine, packaging film and product being processed.

Where applicable, test packs can be examined for sealing quality, bag dimensions, filling consistency, cutting accuracy and coding alignment. Any adjustments must be made within the equipment's operating specifications. Other machinery may require different checks according to its purpose and design.

Commissioning can also help operators become familiar with the machine's normal operating sequence and identify warning signs that may require technical attention. We explain relevant operating considerations and identify outstanding issues that need to be resolved before the equipment is relied upon for routine production.

Our goal is to help clients move from installation to verified machine operation through appropriate setup, testing and technical checks. Production readiness depends on the equipment condition, the product, the facility and any applicable quality and safety requirements.

06
TRAINING

Machine Operator Training & Production Staff Development

Proper operator training helps production teams understand their machines, follow consistent operating procedures and recognize problems that may affect output or packaging quality. ALPH TECH provides practical training to help machine operators and production personnel become more familiar with the equipment they use in their daily work.

Depending on the machine and the agreed training scope, sessions may cover startup and shutdown procedures, control-panel operation, packaging material loading, product changeovers, routine parameter adjustments, production monitoring and basic operating checks. We explain the purpose of important machine settings and how they influence normal operation.

For VFFS packaging machines, training may include monitoring packaging film movement, understanding bag formation, observing sealing and cutting performance, and recognizing common packaging defects. Operators can learn how to identify symptoms such as poor seals, irregular bag dimensions, inconsistent filling and film tracking difficulties.

We also cover appropriate routine cleaning, basic inspections, equipment care and the importance of following manufacturer instructions. Operators are guided on when an issue may be addressed through an approved routine adjustment and when the machine should be stopped and referred to a qualified technician.

For food and beverage production environments, correct cleaning procedures, hygiene practices and product handling are also important considerations. Training content should reflect the equipment design, the product being processed and the facility's applicable procedures.

Our objective is to help businesses build a more capable production team, improve familiarity with equipment, promote consistent operating practices and reduce avoidable errors during production. Training can be arranged around the machine installed and the experience of the personnel who will operate it.

07
REPAIRS

VFFS Packaging Machine Repairs, Fault Diagnosis & Troubleshooting

When a packaging machine develops a fault, production can slow down or stop completely. For manufacturers, this may result in delayed orders, wasted packaging materials and disruption to daily operations. ALPH TECH provides technical troubleshooting and repair support for VFFS packaging machines and other selected packaging and production equipment.

We investigate reported faults to help identify their underlying causes. Depending on the equipment and symptoms, troubleshooting may involve mechanical assemblies, electrical circuits, motors, contactors, relays, switches, sensors, heating elements, temperature controllers, control systems and other relevant components.

VFFS packaging machine problems may include poor or inconsistent sealing, incorrect bag length, film tracking difficulties, cutting faults, temperature fluctuations, irregular filling, sensor errors and unexpected machine stoppages. We assess the symptoms and investigate relevant machine settings, components and operating conditions to determine an appropriate corrective action.

We also support troubleshooting for selected sachet water packaging machines, liquid filling machines, shrink-wrapping machines, coding machines and other production equipment. The specific repair approach depends on the machine model, the nature of the fault, the availability of replacement parts and whether specialist manufacturer support is required.

Our troubleshooting approach focuses on understanding why a problem is occurring rather than simply addressing the visible symptom. This may involve inspecting components, checking electrical and control functions, reviewing machine settings and testing the relevant functions after corrective work.

Electrical and mechanical repairs are carried out according to the equipment and the agreed scope of work. Appropriate isolation and safety procedures are essential before inspection or repair, and work beyond the available technical scope may require a specialist.

If your VFFS packaging machine is not sealing correctly, producing inconsistent packs, stopping unexpectedly or refusing to start, contact ALPH TECH with the machine type, model and symptoms. This information helps us understand the problem and advise on the next step towards diagnosing and resolving the fault.

08
MAINTENANCE

Preventive Maintenance, Machine Servicing & Technical Support

Regular maintenance is important for keeping production equipment in suitable operating condition and identifying developing faults before they cause major disruption. ALPH TECH provides preventive maintenance, servicing and technical support for packaging machinery and selected production equipment used in manufacturing and food and beverage operations.

Maintenance activities depend on the equipment model, its operating hours, production conditions and manufacturer recommendations. They may include inspecting moving components, checking belts and fasteners, cleaning appropriate sensors, inspecting heating assemblies, reviewing electrical connections, checking pneumatic components and verifying relevant machine settings.

For VFFS packaging machines, regular attention to film transport components, forming assemblies, sealing mechanisms, cutting systems, sensors and temperature controls can help identify wear, contamination or performance changes that affect packaging quality. Appropriate maintenance can also help operators recognize when parts require adjustment, repair or replacement.

We can help clients investigate recurring faults, identify equipment components that need attention and establish practical servicing routines. Where appropriate, maintenance planning may include inspection records, routine checks and recommendations based on the equipment's actual condition and operating requirements.

Technical support may also be needed when a machine develops intermittent faults, begins producing inconsistent results or requires attention after extended operation. Our aim is to help clients investigate these issues and determine a suitable response based on the technical assessment.

Preventive maintenance does not eliminate every possible breakdown, but a suitable maintenance programme can help reduce avoidable interruptions, support consistent operation and improve awareness of equipment condition. Maintenance should always follow relevant safety procedures and manufacturer guidance.

Whether you require routine servicing, help with recurring machine problems or advice on maintaining your packaging equipment, ALPH TECH can assist with identifying an appropriate maintenance and technical support plan.
