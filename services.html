OUR SERVICES

Technical Support for Every Stage of Production

Whether you are establishing a new factory, purchasing replacement equipment or improving an existing production line, ALPH TECH provides services tailored to your machinery, operating requirements and business objectives.

01

PLANNING & EXPERT GUIDANCE

Technical Consultation

Choosing machinery without understanding your production requirements can result in unsuitable equipment, unnecessary expenses and avoidable operating difficulties. ALPH TECH provides technical consultation to help businesses assess their needs before purchasing, installing or upgrading production equipment.

We advise on machine selection, production capacity, equipment compatibility, installation requirements, operating procedures and the practical considerations involved in running a production line. Our consultation covers packaging machines, monoblock filling systems, shrink wrappers, PET bottle blowing machines, coding equipment, water treatment systems and food processing machinery.

For new and existing factories, we help identify technical requirements, evaluate possible equipment options and plan the next steps towards efficient production. This service is suitable for entrepreneurs establishing a factory, manufacturers expanding capacity and businesses experiencing recurring machine problems.

02

MACHINE SOURCING & SUPPLY

Industrial Machine Procurement

ALPH TECH assists businesses in sourcing industrial machinery suited to their products, production targets and available facilities. We help clients identify suitable equipment rather than selecting machines based on price alone.

Our machine categories include VFFS and other packaging machines, monoblock filling and capping machines, liquid and paste filling equipment, shrink wrapping machines, PET bottle blowing machines, bottling lines, coding and marking machines, water treatment equipment and food processing machinery.

Depending on the project, we can assist with identifying machine specifications, discussing capacity requirements, checking compatibility with existing equipment and determining the accessories or supporting systems needed for installation. We also consider factors such as the product being processed, packaging format, available utilities and the intended production process.

Our objective is to help clients make informed purchasing decisions and prepare for the installation and operation of their equipment. Procurement support can be arranged for individual machines or for equipment required for a wider production setup.

03

FACTORY PLANNING & INTEGRATION

Factory Setup & Production Line Planning

Setting up a production facility involves more than purchasing machines. Equipment must be arranged properly, utilities must be considered, and each stage of production should work with the next. ALPH TECH provides technical planning support for businesses establishing new factories or reorganising existing production facilities.

We help clients plan machinery placement, production flow, equipment spacing, operator working areas and the practical requirements for installation and servicing. Our work can cover bottled water and beverage facilities, packaging operations, food processing plants and other manufacturing businesses.

Depending on the project, equipment planning may include water treatment systems, PET bottle blowing machines, filling and capping machines, monoblock systems, packaging machines, shrink wrapping equipment, coding machines and food processing machinery. We consider how these machines will work together and what supporting equipment may be required.

Our aim is to help clients prepare a workable production arrangement, reduce avoidable installation difficulties and make provision for routine operation, maintenance and future expansion.

04

MACHINE INSTALLATION & SETUP

Industrial Machine Installation

Correct installation is essential for machine stability, reliable operation and consistent production. ALPH TECH provides installation and setup services for a range of packaging, filling, bottling, wrapping, coding, water treatment and food processing equipment.

Our installation work may include positioning machinery, mechanical assembly, alignment, electrical connections, control checks, basic operating adjustments and connection to compatible supporting equipment. The specific work depends on the machine design, manufacturer requirements and conditions at the client's facility.

We install and set up supported equipment such as VFFS packaging machines, monoblock filling machines, shrink wrappers, PET bottle blowing machines, filling and capping systems, coding and marking machines, water treatment equipment and other production machinery.

We also consider the practical requirements for machine access, operator use and future servicing. Where necessary, we help identify installation requirements that should be addressed before the equipment is put into operation. Our goal is to ensure that the machine is properly set up and ready for the commissioning and testing stage.

05

TESTING & PRODUCTION READINESS

Machine Commissioning & Test Running

After installation, machinery needs to be checked and tested to confirm that its components, controls and operating functions work as expected. ALPH TECH provides commissioning and test-running support to help businesses prepare their equipment for production.

Depending on the machine, this can include checking connections, inspecting operating functions, adjusting suitable parameters, verifying product movement and carrying out trial runs. We help identify operational issues that need attention before normal production begins.

Our commissioning support covers packaging machines, monoblock filling systems, shrink wrapping machines, PET blowing equipment, liquid filling and capping machines, coding systems, water treatment equipment and other supported production machinery.

Where applicable, we assess factors such as packaging consistency, filling accuracy, sealing performance, coding position, bottle formation and machine coordination. The checks carried out depend on the equipment and its intended application. We also help operators understand the relevant controls and normal operating procedures during handover.

06

OPERATOR SKILLS & SAFE OPERATION

Machine Operator Training

Well-trained operators are important for smooth production, consistent product quality and the proper use of industrial equipment. ALPH TECH provides practical machine training to help operators and production staff understand how to use, monitor and care for the machines they operate.

Training can cover machine startup and shutdown, control panel functions, operating settings, product changeovers, routine cleaning, basic fault identification and the correct operating sequence. We also explain the importance of following manufacturer instructions and applicable safety procedures.

We provide training for supported VFFS packaging machines, monoblock fillers, shrink wrapping machines, PET bottle blowing machines, liquid filling and capping equipment, coding machines, water treatment systems and food processing machinery.

Training is adapted to the particular equipment and the experience of the staff. Where appropriate, operators receive practical guidance on recognising common operating problems, knowing when to stop a machine and understanding when qualified technical assistance is required. This helps businesses build operator confidence and manage equipment more effectively.

07

FAULT DIAGNOSIS & REPAIR

Machine Repairs & Troubleshooting

Machine breakdowns can interrupt production, delay deliveries and increase operating costs. ALPH TECH provides troubleshooting and repair services for supported industrial packaging and production equipment. We work to identify the cause of a fault and determine the appropriate corrective action.

Our repair work may involve investigating mechanical, electrical, control and operating problems, inspecting affected components, checking sensors and connections, adjusting machine settings and replacing faulty or worn parts where suitable. The repair process depends on the machine, the nature of the fault and the availability of compatible components.

We support different machine categories, including VFFS packaging machines, monoblock filling machines, shrink wrappers, PET bottle blowing machines, liquid filling and capping systems, coding and marking machines, water treatment equipment and food processing machinery.

Whether a machine is failing to start, producing inconsistent results, experiencing sealing or filling problems, showing control faults or operating below its expected performance, we assess the reported symptoms and work towards a practical solution. Our objective is to help clients restore reliable operation and reduce unnecessary production downtime.

08

PREVENTIVE CARE & AFTER-SALES SUPPORT

Preventive Maintenance & Technical Support

Regular maintenance helps identify developing problems before they lead to major breakdowns. ALPH TECH provides maintenance and technical support for industrial machines used in packaging, filling, bottling, wrapping, coding, water treatment and food processing operations.

Depending on the equipment, maintenance activities may include inspection, cleaning, lubrication where specified, checking fasteners and connections, examining wear components, verifying sensors and reviewing operating performance. We also help identify components that may require attention or replacement.

Our maintenance services cover supported VFFS packaging machines, monoblock fillers, shrink wrapping machines, PET blowing equipment, filling and capping systems, coding machines, water treatment systems and other production machinery.

We can also provide technical guidance when operators experience recurring faults, changes in machine performance or uncertainty about routine care. Where applicable, we help clients understand maintenance intervals and the importance of using suitable spare parts and following manufacturer recommendations.

Our goal is to help businesses care for their equipment, improve operational reliability and make informed maintenance decisions throughout the machine's service life.

EQUIPMENT WE SUPPORT

One Technical Partner for Different Machine Categories

ALPH TECH supports a broad range of industrial machinery. Our services are not limited to one machine type or one production process. The exact work available for each machine depends on its model, technical requirements and the nature of the job.

Packaging & Sachet Packaging Machines

VFFS machines and other vertical packaging systems, powder packaging machines, granular packaging machines, liquid and paste packaging machines, and sachet packaging equipment. Services include procurement support, installation, commissioning, operator training, troubleshooting and maintenance.

Monoblock, Filling & Capping Machines

Monoblock filling machines, liquid filling equipment, bottle filling systems, capping machines and related production equipment. We support machine setup, filling and capping adjustments, testing, operator training, repairs and routine servicing.

Shrink Wrapping & End-of-Line Equipment

Shrink wrapping machines, bundling equipment, sealing machines and related packaging-line equipment. Our services may include installation, conveyor and operating adjustments, commissioning, fault diagnosis, operator training and maintenance.

PET Bottle Blowing & Bottling Equipment

PET bottle blowing machines and related bottle production equipment, alongside compatible filling and bottling systems. Support may include installation, setup, heating and blowing parameter checks, test running, operator training, troubleshooting and maintenance.

Coding, Marking & Labelling Machines

Water Treatment & Beverage Production Equipment

Food Processing & Production Machinery
